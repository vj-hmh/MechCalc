References and assumptions

Bolt Preload and Torque (US)

Yield capacity (lbf) = At (in²) × Sy (ksi) × 1000
Target preload Fi = preload fraction × At × Sy × 1000
Torque T (lbf·in) = K × Fi (lbf) × d (in)
Confirm against approved design basis.
Must be supported by approved lubrication/friction data.

Tensile Stress (US)

Effective load (kip) = Applied load (kip) × distribution factor ÷ number of load-sharing components
σt (ksi) = Effective load (kip) ÷ Tensile area (in²)

Thread Shear Stress (US)

τ = F (kip) ÷ As (in²) — results in ksi
Enter approved shear areas calculated from the applicable thread-form geometry. This prototype deliberately does not infer proprietary or standard thread shear areas.

Bearing Stress (US)

σb (ksi) = Bearing force (kip) ÷ Projected bearing area (in²)

Membrane Stress (US)

σm = F (kip) ÷ A (in²) — result in ksi

Hydraulic Cylinder Force (US)

Acap = πD²/4 (in²)   |   Arod-side = π(D²−d²)/4 (in²)
Fnet (kip) = (Pcap (psi)×Acap − Prod (psi)×Arod-side) × efficiency ÷ 100 ÷ 1000

Utilization-Factor Summary

Stress check UF = Calculated stress (ksi) ÷ Allowable stress (ksi)
Force-capacity UF = Required force (kip) ÷ Available force (kip)
