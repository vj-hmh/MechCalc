Project Information

References and assumptions

Bolt Preload and Torque

Yield capacity = At × Sy
Target preload Fi = preload fraction × At × Sy
Torque T = K × Fi × d
Confirm against approved design basis.
Must be supported by approved lubrication/friction data.

Tensile Stress

Effective load = Applied load × distribution factor ÷ number of load-sharing components
σt = Effective load ÷ Tensile area

Thread Shear Stress

τexternal = F ÷ As,external
τinternal = F ÷ As,internal
Enter approved shear areas calculated from the applicable thread-form geometry. This prototype deliberately does not infer proprietary or standard thread shear areas.

Bearing Stress

σb = Bearing force ÷ Projected bearing area

Membrane Stress

σm = F ÷ A

Hydraulic Cylinder Force

Acap = πD²/4   |   Arod-side = π(D²-d²)/4
Fnet = (PcapAcap - ProdArod-side) × efficiency × cylinders

Utilization-Factor Summary

Stress check UF = Calculated stress ÷ Allowable stress
Force-capacity UF = Required force ÷ Available force
