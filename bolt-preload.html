Bolt Preload and Torque

Yield Load Capacity (Fy) = Tensile Stress Area (At) × Yield Strength (Sy)
Initial Bolt Preload Force (Fi) = Target Preload Fraction (f) × Yield Load Capacity (Fy)
Tightening Torque (T) = Nut Factor (K) × Initial Bolt Preload Force (Fi) × Nominal Bolt Diameter (d)

Prototype engineering aid. Results require independent engineering verification.
